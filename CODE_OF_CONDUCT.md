# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and help keep the project welcoming to everyone,
regardless of background or experience. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to ravindra.suthar@me.com. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may
remove comments, close issues or block participants who don't follow these guidelines.
